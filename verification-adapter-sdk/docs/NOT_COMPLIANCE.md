# This is not a compliance certification

The Universal Open-Source Verification Adapter SDK provides **engineering
primitives**: typed adapter contracts, manifests, webhook verification helpers,
conformance tests, and a CLI.

It is **not**:

- a legal opinion
- a KYC, KYB, CIP, BSA/AML, or GDPR certification
- a substitute for counsel, a compliance officer, or a licensed vendor program
- an attestation that your deployment meets Stripe Identity, Persona, Plaid,
  or any regulator's requirements
- a SplitIn product authorization policy

You are responsible for:

- choosing lawful providers and packages for each jurisdiction
- data retention, redaction, and subject-access requests
- production access control and secret management
- any certifications your business requires

The United States is the **first certified engineering market** for these
packages (sandbox contract coverage). That phrase describes SDK test coverage
and documented adapters. It does not mean regulatory approval.

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [privacy-retention.md](privacy-retention.md).
